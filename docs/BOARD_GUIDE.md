# Board demonstration guide

Suggested duration: 6–8 minutes. Open the public application and select Board view.

1. **Problem:** regulations and industry signals are fragmented. Explain why motorcycle applicability matters more than broad automotive keywords.
2. **Collection:** open Source monitoring. Show supported adapters, real last-check results, blocked sources and planned/manual coverage. Healthy is not the same as comprehensive.
3. **Scoring:** show the five relevance weights and independent confidence/urgency checks. Explain that bills are not current law and unknown dates do not create an urgent obligation.
4. **Real example:** open EVIDA's historical baseline. Show the original source, Section 3 scope reference, P1 relevance and absence of a fabricated near-term deadline. Explain what still requires model-specific legal review.
5. **Applicability trap:** open EO 62. Explain why its body text cannot establish an exact motorcycle tariff rate without Annex A. In the feed, switch to the clearly labelled fictional scenarios to show the restamping and P0 tests.
6. **Institutional response:** show manufacturer/dealership assessments and the recommended committee action. Use Send This to add a record to the persistent review queue. Return to Email center and generate a preview. Use a sandbox approval to demonstrate traceability; it delivers no real email.
7. **Adoption:** propose a supervised pilot with policy/technical reviewers. Validate additional agencies and PDF extraction, then measure coverage, review turnaround, false-positive rate and policy-response lead time.

State the limits candidly: some agencies block automated access; legacy material is labelled historical; legal conclusions and effective dates require verification; no member contact list or production email provider was supplied. Public GitHub Pages is a read-only alternative; review workflow data stays in each browser’s isolated session in the public application.
